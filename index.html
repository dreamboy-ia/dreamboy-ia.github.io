Pipit Learns makes calm learning videos for children aged 2 to 5: letter sounds (phonics), Montessori-inspired lessons, numbers, feelings and everyday skills, taught by Pipit, a little sparrow.

The videos are published on YouTube as Pipit Learns, and in Indonesian on TikTok and Instagram as Belajar Bareng Pipit.

Pipit Learns Publisher is the small internal tool the channel owner uses to upload these videos to the channel owner's own accounts. It is not offered to other users.
